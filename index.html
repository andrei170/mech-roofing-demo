The questions everybody asks.

Your homepage currently has two subheadings. WNC Roofing has seventeen. Every question below becomes one — the same information you already have, arranged so somebody on a phone finds theirs.

How long does a full roof replacement take?

Most single-family replacements are done inside a day or two depending on size, pitch and how much decking needs replacing once the old roof is off.

Repair or replace — how do I know?

Age, the condition of the decking, and whether the problem is one area or the whole roof. We will tell you honestly if a repair buys you several more years.

Will my insurance cover storm damage?

Often, if the damage is genuine and recent. We document it properly and meet the adjuster. We will also tell you when there is not enough damage to be worth a claim.

Do I need to move my cars?

Yes, and anything fragile near the house. We will tell you exactly what to move the day before.

What happens if it rains mid-job?

We only tear off what we can dry in. If weather turns, the roof is covered before anybody leaves site.

Who clears the old shingles and the nails?

We do. Debris goes in the trailer and we run magnets over the yard and driveway before we go.

What warranty do I get, and from whom?

There are two — the manufacturer's on the materials and ours on the workmanship. We will put both in writing before you commit.
